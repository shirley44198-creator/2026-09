<ul>
<li><strong>Confirm the receiving address:</strong> Copy-paste, don't type manually. Some chains (e.g. XRP) require a Memo/Tag — without it, funds won't arrive.</li>
<li><strong>Confirm the network:</strong> USDT on different networks (ERC20 / TRC20 / Arbitrum) are different forms; sender and receiver must match.</li>

<blockquote class="warn">⚠️ When a computer is infected with malware, clipboard addresses can be secretly replaced ("clipboard hijacking"). After pasting an address, always re-check the first and last 4–6 characters.</blockquote>

<p>The OKX Web3 wallet has a built-in DApp browser for connecting to DeFi, GameFi, and other on-chain apps. Connecting triggers an approval request — this authorizes the contract to use specific tokens in your wallet.</p>

<p><strong>Safety principles:</strong></p>

<h2>8. 5 Most Common Web3 Scams for Beginners</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Fake support asking for seed phrase:</strong> Anyone asking for your seed phrase or private key is a scam — no exceptions.</li>
<li><strong>Phishing sites:</strong> Fake DApp front-ends that drain your wallet on signature. Access official sites from bookmarks, not links from strangers.</li>
<li><strong>Airdrop bait:</strong> "Connect wallet to claim airdrop" — actually a malicious approval page.</li>
<li><strong>Clipboard hijacking:</strong> Malware replaces your copied receiving address; always verify first/last characters before transferring.</li>
<li><strong>Fake KOL "signals":</strong> DMs from self-proclaimed "mentors" asking you to transfer assets to a "designated custody wallet."</li>
</ol>
