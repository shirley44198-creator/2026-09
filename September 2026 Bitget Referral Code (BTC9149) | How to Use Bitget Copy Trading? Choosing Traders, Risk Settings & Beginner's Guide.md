<h2>II. What is Bitget Copy Trading?</h2>

<p>The principle of copy trading is: you select a trader, and the system automatically replicates their actions of opening, closing, and adjusting positions in proportion.</p>

<p>What it means for beginners:</p>

<p>No need to watch the market 24/7 — the trader executes the strategy;<br>
No need to judge entry and exit timing yourself;<br>
You can observe multiple traders' public track records before deciding.</p>

<p>But you must set the right expectations first: Copy trading is not capital-guaranteed, nor is it handing your funds over to someone else for safekeeping. <h2>VI. How to Set Copy Trading Risk Controls?</h2>

<p>Before starting to copy, confirm each item one by one:</p>

<p><strong>Copy amount:</strong> Only use funds you can afford to lose. Don't all-in on a single trader;<br>
<strong>Stop-loss line:</strong> Set an account-level stop-loss that auto-stops when triggered;<br>
<strong>Leverage:</strong> Don't blindly copy a trader's high-leverage settings;<br>
<strong>Diversification:</strong> Copy 2-3 traders with different styles at the same time, rather than going all-in on one;<br>
<strong>Regular review:</strong> Check performance weekly. If results deviate from expectations, stop-loss and exit.</p>

<p>⚠️ One of the most common causes of loss is never checking your account after starting to copy. When market conditions shift, yesterday's top trader may experience consecutive drawdowns.</p>
